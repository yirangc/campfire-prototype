# Design flags

Conflicts in the Figma references, choices Figma does not document, accessibility concerns, and what could not be verified. The showcase renders the same list from src/showcase/flags.ts; regenerate this file with `npm run flags:doc` after editing it. For the subset that affects the prototype, with recommendations, see prototype-decisions.md.

## Conflicts between references

- Panel icon (12:12165) is described as 75% artwork but is drawn edge to edge on the 24 px canvas; its 16 px instance (38:5739) also uses a 1 px stroke instead of 1.5 px. The files follow the drawing, including the 1 px stroke at 16 px.
- Primary button color. Foundations (12:10947) says "Primary is neutral ink; lime is a selective accent, not a universal brand fill", and the button specimen is labelled "Primary / neutral", but the Primary button component (12:12176) and every Reconcile screen fill it with accent lime. The build follows the component (lime fill, primary ink label), as asked on 2026-10-04.
- Tab row gaps. The Tab specimen row (31:909) uses 16 px between tabs; the Reconcile status tabs use 12 px (28:8032 and others) and 20 px (28:8614). Tab widths are fixed per instance (88, 96, 132 px) rather than following the label. The shared Tabs component uses 16 px and takes a width per tab.
- Reporting breadcrumb has two sizes. Navigation (12:12175): 12/20 text, 12 px chevron, 10 px gap. Financial reporting header (12:11632): 11/20 text, 10 px chevron, 8 px gap. Both are implemented (Breadcrumbs plain, and plain + compact).
- Metric value tracking. Foundations lists 28/34 semibold with no tracking; the metric cards use −0.8 px. The cards follow the component (−0.8 px).
- Date range control. Controls (12:12136) has a 16 px calendar icon at 300 px wide; the assembled Income Statement (12:11645) has no icon at 296 px. Both are possible with Select (icon prop optional); the Income Statement specimen follows its own frame.
- Table header recipes differ. Income Statement: 42 px, subtle fill, 12 px medium secondary. Allocation: 36 px, white, 12/20 regular secondary. Each table follows its own frame.
- Row anatomy specimens (12:12186, 12:12187) differ from rows inside the Income Statement: 12 px vs 8 px chevron gap, 11/20 vs 11/16 label, full border vs bottom rule, fixed 150 px amount. FinancialTable follows the assembled table.
- Allocation total. Inside the table: 38 px, "Total" at 200 px from the left, 14 px gap. Standalone specimen (12:12198): 44 px, 12 px inset, 16 px gap. Both are implemented.
- Allocation Percentage heading has no right inset, so it sits 12 px right of the values beneath it. Implemented as drawn; it breaks the "shared right edge" rule stated for financial tables.
- Spacing scale documents 4–48 but components bind 2, 6, 10, 14 and 20 (and raw 3 px and 40 px gaps). All are tokens; the off-scale steps are marked in Foundations.
- The side panels embed a "Campfire logo reconstruction" image while the identity page uses the "supplied high-resolution" logo. The build uses the supplied 4x exports of the identity layers (12:12168, 12:12169) everywhere.
- The full-page render of the navigation frame shows "Contracts" (unindented) where the Reporting side panel component contains "Cash Flow". The component and its isolated render agree on Cash Flow, which is what is built.
- The dropdown open menu is 260 px in Figma while the closed Cadence control is 160 px. The menu here is at least as wide as its trigger.
- Reconcile data. The Figma frames show an eight-transaction November (Stripe payout to Monthly bank service fee) with a $220 or $15 difference and "John Glasgow" as the user. The prototype uses the PRD dataset instead (24 records, $320 initial difference) and Maya, because the PRD takes precedence. Five of the Figma rows are the PRD's background pairs. The PRD treated them as already reviewed; at Yirang's request (2026-10-04) they are Auto-matched items in the main table that still need review, so all 24 records count toward progress and completion.
- Reconcile register heading and counts. The register is titled "Transactions" (Figma and Yirang's 2026-10-04 design) and every tab count is a record count over the 24 original records, derived from the current state; generated entries are never counted. Tabs, in the design's order: All (24), Auto-matched (10), Suggested (6), Unmatched (8), Confirmed, Outstanding. Each tab lists only cases with its own status and counts only those records, as the PRD splits them (6 / 8): Unmatched no longer includes suggested pairs (Yirang, 2026-10-05, replacing her 2026-10-04 request). Suggested and auto-matched records are not unmatched, but they block completion until confirmed or dismissed. The status filter dropdown is removed because the design has only the search field and the tabs. The line above the table reads "Showing 24 rows · 12 bank transactions · 12 ledger entries" (Yirang's format, 2026-10-05): all three counts come from the rows on screen after the tab and the search, created entries included, and update with every change. It has no overall totals.
- Auto-matched pairs (Yirang's design, 2026-10-04). The five PRD background pairs are Auto-matched cases in the main table, with a blue "Auto-matched" pill (link ink on a new light-blue fill, #E3ECFC, sampled from the design; green stays for user-approved matches). The design's link glyph is not in the Figma icon set, so the pill shows its label only. The expanded row reuses the suggested-match layout: a plain "Automatically matched · Awaiting your review" heading over "The amount, date, and description match. Review the details below, then confirm." (Yirang's wording), bank card left, "Ledger entry" card right, and Leave Unresolved, Dismiss suggestion and Confirm match with the same behavior and Undo. Dismissing makes both records Unmatched and searchable. The design's bank text "STRIPE PAYOUT" and reference STR-1103 are not adopted: the PRD gives these pairs no references, so the fixture descriptions stay as they were.
- Expanded-row comparison (51:1049) without the arrow, at Yirang's request (2026-10-04). Figma puts a 32 px chevron column between the bank and ledger cards; it is removed in every expanded state, and the two cards sit 12 px apart at equal widths. Side by side, the cards share row tracks (CSS subgrid), so the amount, date, description and reference start at the same height and column in both cards, and the bank card lists Reference before Type (with "—" when there is none) to match the ledger card's order. Fields sit in four columns when each card has room for four 104 px fields and in two otherwise. The row expand and collapse chevrons are unchanged.
- Ledger cards without "Match status" (Yirang, 2026-10-04). The suggested, auto-matched and matched ledger cards no longer repeat the status already shown by the row pill and the notice; the slot shows the entry's counterparty instead, a field the ledger data already has and the ledger-only card already shows. Created entries show nothing extra, since their counterparty is their category.
- Cleared balance with auto-matched pairs. Only confirmed matches clear, so an unconfirmed auto-matched pair no longer counts as cleared: the cleared balance starts at the $100,000.00 opening balance instead of the PRD's $109,870.00 and rises as pairs are confirmed. Statement closing ($109,650.00), book balance ($109,970.00) and the $320.00 difference are unchanged, and the finish is the same ($109,650.00 cleared, $0.00 difference) with 21 Confirmed and 3 Outstanding. A save completed before this change is reopened on load, with every decision kept and the reopening recorded in history.
- Suggested-match ledger memos. GL-1101 reads "ACH Northstar Hosting" and GL-1105 "DELTA PAY receipt" instead of the PRD's "Northstar hosting" and "Delta receipt", so the two correct suggestions differ from their bank lines only by the shortened payee or one word, plus the 1-day date gap (Yirang's request, 2026-10-04). Amounts, dates, references and the deliberately wrong Alder/Birch suggestion are unchanged.
- One row per record (Yirang, 2026-10-05). The Figma register (49:617, 28:8442) puts a matched or suggested pair on one row with both amounts and the ledger entry. The prototype gives every bank transaction and ledger entry its own row: a bank row fills only Bank amount, a ledger row fills only Ledger amount and Ledger entry / date. The two rows of a pair share the pair's status, change together on confirm, dismiss and Undo, and expanding either one shows the same match details; on the same date they sit together, bank row first. Entries created from a bank line get their own ledger row (tagged Created) but are not counted, so the Confirmed tab can show more rows than its record count. Each row button's accessible name starts with "Bank transaction" or "Ledger entry", since a bank line and its entry can share a date and description. Balances come from the records, not the rows, so nothing is counted twice.
- Reconcile register columns. 49:617 has five columns; 28:8442 adds an Action column with an Undo link. At Yirang's request (2026-10-05) the prototype follows 49:617: no Action column, and Undo sits at the right of the expanded row's banner ("Match confirmed", "Created … and matched it", "Documented as outstanding").
- Reconcile control heights. The expense form draws its dropdown and inputs at 34 px (38:7885, 38:7939, 38:8005); the controls page draws 36 px dropdowns and 38 px fields. Both are built (size="compact" on Select and TextField).
- "Paid from" (PRD) vs "Payment account" (Figma 38:7927) on the expense form. The PRD label is used.
- Completed summary (28:8924) shows "8 of 8 transactions" and a single Bank Fees adjustment. It is updated per the PRD: records out of 24 (14 PRD exceptions and the 10 auto-matched records), confirmed and outstanding counts, the three created entries, and the outstanding adjustment (+$1,000.00 / −$800.00 / net +$200.00) with its evidence.

## Undocumented choices

- Icon color by state. Icons use color/text/secondary everywhere, as bound in the components. Figma check instances 28:8846 and 28:8918 are green (#3E7461) and x instance 38:7385 is blue (#1A65F8), with no stated rule.
- Settings is now a filled gear (no stroke), the only filled icon in an outline set. Lock (38:7923) was added to the icon grid without an observed or suggested label, and its description is empty.
- Hover, pressed and disabled states for buttons, tabs, nav rows and inputs. Hover reuses surface/subtle, as the Tab hover variant does. There is no token for a lime primary hover, so primary hover shows no change. Disabled uses muted ink (tabs and buttons alike).
- Keyboard focus outside text inputs. Figma draws focus only on the text field (Field/Focus, 12:12184): a 2 px focus-lime stroke inside and a 2 px lime ring at 50% outside. The same recipe is applied to buttons, tabs and every other focusable element. In the reconcile register the ring wraps the whole row, and after a row is opened or closed it stays on that row only while the row keeps focus: clicking anywhere else clears it, as it clears the search field and dropdown highlights, and an open dropdown or search list closes, while expanded rows and search text stay (Yirang, 2026-10-05). Tab still shows the ring.
- Scrollbars (Yirang, 2026-10-05; both apps, src/index.css and src/scrollbars.ts). The page always reserves its scrollbar gutter (scrollbar-gutter: stable), so a scrollbar appearing never moves the layout. Classic, always-on scrollbars hide while idle and show while scrolling, by wheel, keyboard or drag, or when the pointer is within 24 px of a scroll area's edge, fading after 1 s. Only the thumb color changes (gray/400 on a clear track, since Figma draws no scrollbar), so width, dragging and keyboard scrolling stay native. Overlay scrollbars, browsers without scrollbar-color (Safari), forced colors and "more contrast" are left to the system. The allocation table keeps its permanent Figma track.
- Status pill tooltips (Yirang, 2026-10-05). Each register pill explains its status on hover or keyboard focus, using the hidden tooltip recipe (49:800) through the shared Tooltip component. The wording for Suggested, Unmatched, Confirmed and Auto-matched is Yirang's; Outstanding ("You’ve documented this as a timing difference that clears after the statement period.") is proposed. The pill becomes a tab stop, the tooltip opens below it aligned to its right edge, never takes the pointer, hides with Escape, and passes clicks to the row: one click on the pill expands or collapses the row once, with the tooltip still showing (Yirang, 2026-10-05). Figma has no tooltip interaction or delay, so it shows immediately.
- Selected value styling inside dropdown cells (Figma shows placeholders only): primary ink, regular weight.
- Menu option highlight vs selection: Figma's open menu shows one row with both a subtle fill and a check. Here the fill follows the keyboard/pointer highlight and the check marks the selected value.
- Menu offset from its trigger (4 px) and its z-index.
- Child rows in the Income Statement (label indented to align with the parent label). Figma shows no child rows.
- Cleared department cell, allocation totals other than 100.00%, and "Collapse All" as the pressed Expand All label.
- Minimum width of the allocation table before it scrolls (960 px).
- Child labels for nav groups that are collapsed in Figma (Financial Statements, Accounting and Cash Management on the dashboard panel) are sample labels.
- Negative or neutral trend badges are not documented and are not built.
- Reconcile design additions (PRD "Design addition"; no Figma frame): the Outstanding pill and tab, the outstanding-item form with December evidence, the search empty-results line, the separate-expense acknowledgement, the "Created" chip on generated entries, the save-failure notice, the blocked-completion notice, Reopen, the unreadable-data recovery screen and the demo reset dialog. All reuse existing tokens and components; none adds a color.
- Find another ledger entry follows Figma 38:6512, 38:7275 and 38:7320 at Yirang's request (2026-10-04): heading, subtitle, Create entry… and one search field. Focusing it lists every unmatched record on the other side, whatever its amount, with same-amount records first (Yirang, 2026-10-04); typing narrows the list by description, reference, entry id, date (Nov 17, November 17, 11/17, 2025-11-17) or amount (12,500, 12500, $12,500.00, -450). The placeholder reads "Search by keywords, date or amount" (Figma: "Search by keywords or date") and the subtitle says the same. The PRD's visible date and counterparty filters, keyword suggestions and matching-criteria text are removed. The match rules (same signed amount, currency and account) are enforced on Confirm match: choosing a record with a different amount shows why it cannot be matched, and nothing changes. With no results the menu shows "No matching ledger entries" (no Figma state).
- Leave Unresolved only collapses the row, at Yirang's request (2026-10-04). The row stays in place with its Unmatched or Suggested status, keeps any draft, and still blocks completion. No note, status, tab, banner, history entry or confirmation is added, which departs from the PRD's optional note.
- Responsive layout, at Yirang's request (2026-10-04). Figma has desktop frames only, so every narrower layout is a design addition built from existing tokens and components, with no new color or type size. Layout follows the width the page actually has (container queries), so it also adapts inside a narrow artifact frame. Below 1100 px the sidebar becomes a menu drawer opened from a "Menu" button (panel icon) in the top bar. The header actions and account selectors wrap, balance cards go to two columns under 960 px and one under 480 px, and the register uses narrower fixed columns under 880 px and compact labeled rows under 640 px, with the expanded detail still directly beneath its row. The detail cards stack (source record first) under 760 px, card fields wrap to as many 104 px columns as fit, form rows drop to one column, and under 480 px the actions stack full width with the primary action first. Dropdowns and search results stay on screen: they open upward when there is no room below, scroll when long, and wrap long options. At 1440 px the page renders exactly as before.
- Field and dropdown error state: warning-ink border with an 11/16 warning-ink message under the field. Figma has no error state, so no new color is introduced.
- Ledger-only cases (outstanding checks, deposits and transfers) open with the ledger entry on the left and the search or outstanding form on the right, mirroring the bank-side layout.
- Undo scope. The History section was removed at Yirang's request (2026-10-04); actions are still recorded for the CSV summary. Each reviewed row's banner offers Undo for that row's own accounting action (match, create and match, outstanding), even after later actions elsewhere; the PRD's strict reverse order is relaxed because each record belongs to at most one action, so undoing one never affects another (Yirang, 2026-10-05). A dismissed suggestion is restored from its own compact notice, as Figma draws it (38:6668).

## Accessibility concerns in the designs

- Focus is back to Figma's lime, as asked on 2026-10-04 (it replaces the chart-green outline added earlier the same day). Focus lime #BCEFA3 is 1.31:1 on white and 1.24:1 on the sidebar, below the 3:1 WCAG 2.2 asks of focus indicators, and on the lime primary button (#B3ED92) the focus stroke is almost invisible (about 1.0:1). Switching --color-focus back to chart green #287D60 (5.0:1 on white) is a one-line change in src/tokens/tokens.css.
- Muted ink (#999D99) is 2.75:1 on white. Placeholders such as "Select Tag" fail WCAG AA text contrast (4.5:1).
- Secondary ink (#727572) is 4.19:1 on the subtle header fill (#F2F3F2), so Income Statement period headings fail AA. It passes on white (4.66:1).
- Selected nav rows differ from the sidebar only by fill (lime 1.29:1, neutral 1.11:1) plus a weight change. aria-current="page" is set for assistive tech, but sighted users get a weak cue, especially for neutral selection.
- Allocation row actions are 12 px icons 8 px apart. Hit areas are extended to 20 px, but WCAG 2.5.8 asks for 24 px targets or spacing; the design does not leave room.
- Every icon-only control (save, delete, close, edit, remove, clear) has no visible label. Accessible names are supplied in code.
- The trend badge communicates direction by color and arrow only; a visually hidden "Up" is added.
- Long nav labels truncate ("New Intercompany Journ..."). The full label is kept in the title attribute and accessible name.
- Input borders (#E7E9E7, 1.22:1) fail the 3:1 non-text contrast guideline for identifying form fields.
- Link blue #1A65F8 on the Auto-matched pill fill #E3ECFC is 4.15:1, under 4.5:1 for the 11 px label, as drawn in Yirang's design. A darker blue ink would fix it but is not in the palette.
- Warning ink #B45309 on the warning pill fill #FFF1C9 is 4.47:1, just under 4.5:1 for the 11 px "Unmatched" label. Warning ink on the notice tint (#FFF8EC) passes at 4.76:1, AI ink passes on both AI fills (5.10:1 and 4.72:1) and the link blue passes on white (4.93:1). Using warning-ink-strong #92400E on the pill (6.31:1) would fix it.

## Not verified

- Logo and mark are the 4x PNG exports Yirang supplied on 2026-10-04 (552 × 128 and 104 × 128). They are renders of the layers, not the original image fills, so they are sharp up to 4x.
- Icons are rebuilt from the Figma vector data and match Figma's 4x renders of all 26 components (after the 2026-10-04 icon update) to within 1% of inked pixels. Five small instances were checked (check 14 and 16, x 16, panel 16, search 12): all within 1% except search 12, whose handle Figma draws about 0.25 px higher. Sizes 10 px and the remaining 12 to 16 px combinations have no Figma instance to compare against.
- Inter rendering differences between Figma and Chromium. Measured: the two reporting breadcrumbs render about 2 px narrower than Figma (190 vs 192, 171 vs 173); every other measured box is within 1 px.
- The allocation table's 8 px scroll track is the browser's native scrollbar, styled in Chromium and Firefox. Systems with overlay scrollbars (macOS default, touch devices) hide the track, so the table renders 8 px shorter there.
- Rendering in Safari and Firefox. Checks ran in Chromium only.
